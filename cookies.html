Cookie Policy.

Updated September 16, 2026

This website uses browser storage for saved projects and preferences, and Google Analytics cookies to measure public-site activity.

Your analytics choice

Analytics is on by default on the public showcase. You can turn it off using Open Cookie Settings. The setting applies to this browser. Turning analytics off reloads the page to stop the Google script already loaded during the visit. It does not delete information Google previously received.

Cookies used by the showcase

Website cookie purposes and lifetimes
CookiePurposeLifetime
_gaGoogle Analytics browser identifier.Up to 180 days, refreshed on visits while analytics is active.

Google’s standard cookie duration can differ from this website’s configured duration. The integration disables advertising storage and personalization. Learn more about Google Analytics cookies.

Local and session storage

  • Saved work: selected projects, named collections and draft cover fields remain in local storage until changed or cleared.
  • Preferences: motion, analytics choice and the optional staff-browser setting remain until changed or cleared.
  • Campaign categories: approved general attribution labels use session storage and expire after 30 minutes without refresh.
  • Administrator sign-in: the separate authorized-materials workspace stores its session for the browser tab. Sign out when finished.

Managing storage

Your browser can block or remove cookies and website storage. Clearing storage removes saved collections, drafts and preferences from that browser. If both cookie and local-storage persistence are blocked, a preference may not survive a later visit. Reading, searching and downloading remain available when analytics is blocked.

Local previews, internal editions, administrator pages and downloaded offline portfolios do not load this public-site analytics integration. Hosting and resource-delivery requests still occur when you open online pages, including when analytics is off.

For more information, read the Privacy Policy. For questions about this website, email adelacruz@northstar.com.
