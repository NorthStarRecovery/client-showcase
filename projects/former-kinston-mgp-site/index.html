Former Kinston Manufactured Gas Plant

Kinston, North Carolina / November 2013–May 2015

River sediment removal and soil stabilization were integrated into a capped site remedy. NorthStar used a cofferdam and barges for dredging, transferred material to the treatment area, mixed soils and sediments with cement and slag, and installed the final cap.

Soils and sediments stabilized
62,000 CY
Approximate river sediment removal
2,400 CY
Average stabilization depth
18–22 ft

The challenge

Connect river-sediment removal with a large land-based stabilization program and final site capping.

NorthStar’s response

NorthStar used a cofferdam and barges for dredging, transferred material to the treatment area, mixed soils and sediments with cement and slag, and installed the final cap.

The result

The profile reports 62,000 cubic yards treated to the required performance criteria before capping.

Project services

  • Sediment Remediation
  • Cofferdam Construction
  • In-Situ Stabilization
  • Environmental Capping
  • Site Restoration

Project context

North Carolina DEQ's waste-management reporting identifies the Kinston Coal Gas Plant in its contaminated-site inventory. This supplies site context rather than evidence of unrestricted closure. The treatment quantities, schedule and safety record in this case study are specifically those reported in NorthStar's construction profile.
