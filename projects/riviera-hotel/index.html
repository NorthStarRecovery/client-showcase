The challenge

Clearing the Riviera Hotel and Casino's 26-acre campus required several demolition methods and hazardous-material controls within a prominent Las Vegas Strip location.

NorthStar’s response

NorthStar removed hazardous materials before structural demolition, using contained work areas and dust management. Excavator teams systematically dismantled the lower structures. Controlled Demolition, Inc. performed the tower implosions in coordination with NorthStar's demolition program.

The result

Four buildings were imploded and 17 others were demolished, clearing the former hotel campus for the Las Vegas Convention Center expansion. The April–September 2016 project combined abatement, mechanical dismantling and specialist implosion work, with zero OSHA recordables.

Project services

  • Demolition
  • Abatement

Project context

Public project background: The Las Vegas Convention and Visitors Authority confirms the Monaco tower implosion on June 14, 2016 and identifies the site's planned use for convention-center expansion.
