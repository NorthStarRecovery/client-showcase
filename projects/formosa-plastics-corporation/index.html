Formosa Plastics Corporation

Illiopolis, Illinois

Hazardous-material response in a damaged chemical plant. NorthStar's scope included emergency debris cleanup, shoring of damaged buildings, hazardous-material handling and recovery of radioactive sources. Potential asbestos and chemical contamination shaped the approach. ATC performed sampling to identify contaminants and recommend remediation protocols, including protective equipment and waste-disposal requirements.

Project value
$5M
OSHA recordables reported
Zero

The challenge

An explosion and fire at the Formosa Plastics plant in Illiopolis left damaged structures, scattered debris and uncertain chemical hazards. Materials required careful assessment before removal or disposal.

NorthStar’s response

NorthStar's scope included emergency debris cleanup, shoring of damaged buildings, hazardous-material handling and recovery of radioactive sources. Potential asbestos and chemical contamination shaped the approach. ATC performed sampling to identify contaminants and recommend remediation protocols, including protective equipment and waste-disposal requirements.

The result

The scope brought debris removal, source recovery and structural shoring together with chemical assessment, protective-equipment planning and waste-disposal controls within the damaged plant.

Project services

  • Demolition
  • Abatement
  • Environmental Remediation
  • Recycling & Reuse

Project context

The U.S. Chemical Safety Board dates the Illiopolis explosion to April 23, 2004, in a PVC production unit. Its investigation provides background on the event.
