The challenge

Westinghouse's former Hematite fuel-manufacturing facility required removal of contaminated equipment, radioactive waste and accumulated hazardous chemicals. Nuclear criticality and radiation-protection requirements governed the work.

NorthStar’s response

NorthStar/LVI, a NorthStar predecessor, removed process equipment and components, segregated and reduced materials, and packaged wastes for licensed disposal or recycling. Radiation-protection support and nuclear-safety evaluations addressed recovery of special nuclear material from the process buildings.

The result

The equipment-removal project finished without a lost-workday incident and earned a reported $50,000 safety bonus. At demobilization in January 2006, the license amendment for demolition to slab had not been approved; Westinghouse deferred that demolition. This engagement therefore does not represent completed building demolition or final site release.

Project services

  • Emergency Response
  • Water Mitigation
  • Demolition
  • Environmental Remediation
  • Reconstruction & Restoration
  • Decommissioning
  • Recycling & Reuse

Project context

Public regulatory background: The NRC's Hematite license-termination notice was issued September 26, 2018, following later site decommissioning. That regulatory milestone is separate from the 2003–2006 equipment-removal engagement. LVI and NCM announced their combination to form NorthStar in April 2014.
