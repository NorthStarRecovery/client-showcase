NORTHSTAR / WEBSITE INFORMATION

Privacy Policy.

Updated September 16, 2026

This notice explains the practices of the NorthStar Client Showcase: our case-study collection, resource library and related website tools.

NorthStar Group Services, Inc., doing business as NorthStar, publishes a corporate Privacy Policy. This page provides additional, website-specific information about the showcase. It does not describe every NorthStar service or replace a separate service agreement.

Information used by this website

Website requests disclose technical information to the services delivering the site, including your IP address, requested address and browser information. GitHub Pages hosts the public website. Its processing is described in GitHub’s privacy statement.

The resource library uses Supabase to deliver published materials and to authenticate authorized administrators. Public resource requests are sent to that service. Administrator sign-in sends credentials to the authentication service; the administrator workspace does not load this website’s Google Analytics integration.

Automatic website analytics

The public showcase loads Google Analytics automatically unless you turn it off in Cookie Settings. Analytics measures page and project views, broad industry selections, resource-download clicks, portfolio actions, contact-link clicks and engagement milestones. It also receives browser/device information, general traffic sources and approximate location information.

The integration does not send the words you type into search, personalized portfolio fields, names, email addresses or phone numbers as analytics event parameters. Measurement page addresses omit query strings and fragments. Approved campaign labels are limited to general categories. Advertising storage, advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled. These measures do not make website requests anonymous.

A download click does not confirm that a document was saved or read. Viewing time does not establish comprehension or identify a visitor’s employer. Direct PDF requests and offline copies are not measured by this website’s analytics code. Local previews and internal editions do not load Google Analytics.

Saved projects and browser storage

Saved project selections, named collections and draft cover fields remain in your browser’s local storage until changed or cleared. This includes the recipient, introduction and contact details you choose to enter. Motion preferences, analytics choices and an optional staff-browser classification also use browser storage. These settings do not synchronize across devices.

Shared collection links contain public project identifiers and their order. Copied search-view links contain the search query and filters. Downloaded portfolios contain the personalized fields you choose to include. Anyone receiving those links or files can see the information they contain.

Contacting us

Email links open your email application. Enquiry controls prepare a brief; they do not automatically send a message. If you send a message or submit a separate NorthStar contact form, NorthStar receives what you provide. Please include only information needed for your question.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can turn analytics off or on through Cookie Settings, manage cookies in your browser, and clear saved website data. Turning analytics off stops future collection by this integration; it does not remove information already received by Google. Clearing site data also removes stored preferences and may restore the website’s default settings.

For privacy questions, contact supportcenter@northstar.com. To request access, correction or deletion where applicable, use NorthStar’s privacy request form or the contacts in its corporate policy. For showcase questions and corrections, contact adelacruz@northstar.com.

External services and updates

Read Google’s Privacy Policy, Google’s explanation of partner-site data and Supabase’s Privacy Policy for information about those providers. Links to other sites lead to services with their own practices. We update this notice when the showcase’s practices change.
