Privacy and analytics.

This page explains website measurement on the NorthStar Client Showcase. When connected to NorthStar's Google Analytics property, the public website automatically measures visits and content activity to help us understand which information is useful and how visitors find it.

Local previews, internal editions and downloaded offline portfolios do not use this analytics integration. Staff using the public showcase can explicitly mark their browser as staff; those visits remain in reporting as a separate audience.

What analytics measures.

  • Page visits, case studies viewed and industry selections.
  • Marketing materials opened and clicks on PDF or collection downloads.
  • Portfolio creation actions, print requests and clicks to contact NorthStar.
  • Time milestones while a case study or HTML flyer is active in the foreground, content depth reached, expanded project stories and enlarged project images.
  • A staff or unclassified audience label, based only on an explicit browser setting. Unclassified does not mean a confirmed external visitor.
  • Broad traffic sources, approved campaign labels, browser and device information, and approximate geographic information reported by Google Analytics.

Google Analytics can use first-party cookies to distinguish browsers and visits. These browser identifiers do not tell us your name. This integration sets analytics cookies to expire after up to 180 days, with the expiration refreshed on visits. Advertising storage, advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled.

What we do not send.

We do not send names, email addresses, phone numbers, search text, custom portfolio titles, collection names, recipient details or other text you enter to Google Analytics. Page addresses sent by this integration exclude query strings and fragments. Campaign attribution uses a reviewed list of general labels, such as an industry or newsletter, rather than individual recipient labels.

The reporting does not automatically identify a visitor's name, employer or email address. A download click does not prove that a file was saved or read, and viewing time or content depth does not prove reading or comprehension. Direct visits to a PDF and copies opened offline are not measured by the website's analytics code.

Browser storage.

The separate marketing administrator workspace uses Supabase to authenticate approved accounts and store uploaded materials. Sign-in sends your email and password to the authentication service. That workspace does not load Google Analytics. Its sign-in session is kept for the browser tab; sign out when finished.

Publishing an uploaded material makes its document, cover, description, category, keywords and extracted searchable text available to visitors. Drafts are restricted to administrators. Public material views and downloads are measured using a material identifier, without sending the uploaded title, document text or administrator email to Google Analytics.

You can manage cookies and site storage through your browser. An explicit staff setting is kept in this browser's local storage until changed or cleared; it is not an identity or an account login. Browser restrictions or blocking software may prevent analytics from loading. The website's reading, download and portfolio features continue to work if analytics is unavailable. Clearing browser data does not remove information previously processed by Google Analytics.

Google and external destinations.

When the integration is active, measurement information is sent to Google. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

This explanation covers the Client Showcase's analytics. GitHub Pages hosts the site, and hosting requests are subject to GitHub's privacy statement. Contact links take you to NorthStar's separate website or your phone application; those destinations have their own practices.
