Privacy and analytics.

This page explains optional website measurement on the NorthStar Client Showcase. You can browse case studies, read materials and download files without allowing analytics.

Your choice comes first.

When analytics is enabled for this website, we ask before loading Google Analytics. Choosing Decline analytics keeps it off. Choosing Allow analytics lets us understand which content is useful and how visitors find it.

The Analytics settings control at the bottom of each page lets you change your choice. We save that choice in this browser for up to 180 days when browser storage is available. If storage is unavailable, your choice applies to the current page visit.

If you do not see Analytics settings, optional analytics is not active on this version of the website. Local previews, internal views and downloaded offline portfolios do not use this analytics integration.

What analytics measures.

  • Page visits, case studies viewed and industry selections.
  • Marketing materials opened and clicks on PDF or collection downloads.
  • Portfolio creation actions, print requests and clicks to contact NorthStar.
  • Broad traffic sources, approved campaign labels, browser and device information, and approximate geographic information reported by Google Analytics.

After you allow analytics, Google Analytics can use first-party cookies to distinguish browsers and visits. These browser identifiers do not tell us your name. Advertising storage, advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled in this integration.

What we do not send.

We do not send names, email addresses, phone numbers, search text, custom portfolio titles, collection names, recipient details or other text you enter to Google Analytics. Page addresses sent by this integration exclude query strings and fragments. Campaign attribution uses a reviewed list of general labels, such as an industry or newsletter, rather than individual recipient labels.

The reporting does not automatically identify a visitor's name, employer or email address. A download click also does not prove that a file was saved or read. Direct visits to a PDF and copies opened offline are not measured by the website's analytics code.

Changing your choice.

Open Analytics settings and choose Decline analytics to turn it off. This clears the analytics cookies this site can access and reloads the page to stop the already-loaded Google tag. This does not remove information previously processed by Google Analytics. You can also manage cookies and site storage in your browser.

Google and external destinations.

When analytics is allowed, measurement information is sent to Google. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

This explanation covers the Client Showcase's optional analytics. GitHub Pages hosts the site, and hosting requests are subject to GitHub's privacy statement. Contact links take you to NorthStar's separate website or your phone application; those destinations have their own practices.
