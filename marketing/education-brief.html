Education / From scope to handoff

A coordinated path through the work.

Define the physical recovery scope, connect the responsible teams and agree on the decisions needed at each stage.

  1. 01

    Prepare by building

    Record priority functions, site contacts and access requirements before an incident through pre-loss planning.

  2. 02

    Assess the affected areas

    Evaluate physical damage and define mitigation priorities with the facilities team and authorized decision-makers.

  3. 03

    Coordinate the recovery work

    Agree on work areas, sequencing and updates so campus leaders can plan around the restoration effort.

  4. 04

    Restore and document

    Complete the agreed repairs and provide project updates. The institution and relevant authorities determine reopening.

The people behind the decisions.

Facilities and operations

Define building conditions, utilities, access and the spaces needed to support essential functions.

Campus or district leadership

Set operating priorities and coordinate decisions affecting instruction and student services.

Risk and procurement

Confirm work authorization, reporting needs, contract arrangements and insurance coordination.
