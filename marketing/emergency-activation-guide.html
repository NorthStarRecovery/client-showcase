your property response plan

Make the first conversation count.

Gather what is available. These details help NorthStar understand the loss and coordinate with your authorized property representative.

01

Your contact details

First and last name; primary and secondary phone numbers. Include the site contact’s name and both phone numbers.

02

The property

Property name, full address and building type. Add any key contact’s name and phone number.

03

Authorization

Work order or reference number, if available. Otherwise, provide the property and authorized representative names.

04

The loss

Source or type of loss; affected building elements, equipment, contents and finishes; approximate affected area.

05

Current conditions

Additional loss details, site conditions, access instructions and any specific response needs.

06

Stay connected

Confirm the authorized contact for arrival estimates, site updates and response coordination.

What happens
next.

The call center notifies management. Operations reviews conditions and coordinates resources. On site, the team meets your representative, updates conditions and scope, begins mitigation, then develops the recovery scope.

Water, fire & contents

Extraction, structural drying, contents protection and recovery, odor controls, duct cleaning, and fire and water restoration.

Environmental response

Biological and chemical remediation, bloodborne pathogen cleanup, asbestos and lead abatement, and biohazard security.

Temporary infrastructure

Heating, cooling, power and lighting; temporary housing and office facilities.

Property recovery

Storm and catastrophe cleanup, snow removal and disposal, demolition, fireproofing and reconstruction.
