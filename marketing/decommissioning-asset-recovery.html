Decommissioning / Service brief

Plan the exit. Preserve the options.

A facility transition involves assets, materials, utilities and future use. NorthStar connects decommissioning, dismantlement and recovery into an accountable scope.

What leaves. What stays. What follows.

The right plan distinguishes equipment for recovery from materials for removal. It also defines utility interfaces, environmental work and the condition needed for the next phase.

Capabilities / Scope planning

Connect each workstream to the transition.

Use one scope discussion to identify dependencies. Establish the appropriate NorthStar delivery team and technical specialists for each facility, material and regulatory context.

Dismantle and recover

Plan equipment removal, rigging, salvage and asset-recovery needs against the owner’s retention decisions.

Integrate environmental work

Connect abatement, decontamination and material handling with the dismantlement sequence.

Protect the interfaces

Identify retained utilities, neighboring operations and the information needed to authorize each transition.
