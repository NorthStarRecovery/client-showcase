ProLogis / FedEx: preparing the next site

100days for demolition and remediation
ProLogis / FedEx Facility | 2020

At the ProLogis / FedEx facility in 2020, demolition and remediation covered two tornado-damaged buildings totaling 1.15 million square feet. The 100-day milestone excludes full rebuilding of those facilities.

01

The challenge

A March 2020 tornado damaged two buildings supporting FedEx operations in Mount Juliet, Tennessee. The combined 1.15-million-square-foot site required demolition and remediation, while salvageable sortation units needed to be recovered and put to use at another facility.

02

The work

NorthStar worked two twelve-hour shifts and brought Sigma Engineering Solutions into daily structural reviews. Crews dismantled, cataloged, crated and relocated 12 sortation units, then rebuilt them at another ProLogis facility. Demolition and remediation included concrete processing and reinstatement to site specifications, with engineering oversight throughout the work.

03

The milestone

Demolition and remediation were completed in 100 days. Relocated sortation units supported logistics operations at another facility, while the original site was prepared for rebuilding. Full rebuilding was a separate stage.

Before the next interruption

Make the site's end condition explicit.

Bring site leadership, EHS and the relevant engineering specialists to the table. Review retained operations, available material information and the condition the owner expects when the agreed work is complete.

  1. Retained operations

    Which utilities, access routes and assets must remain available, and who authorizes changes to them?

  2. Material information

    What surveys, hazard records and disposal requirements are available for the affected structures and materials?

  3. Completion criteria

    What must be removed, restored or left ready for the site's next stage?
