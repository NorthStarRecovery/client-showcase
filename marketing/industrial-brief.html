Industrial / From scope to handoff

A coordinated path through the work.

Define the physical recovery scope, connect the responsible teams and agree on the decisions needed at each stage.

  1. 01

    Define the intended outcome

    Establish the affected areas, retained assets, known hazards and the owner's required condition at completion.

  2. 02

    Plan the site interfaces

    Coordinate access, work boundaries and handoffs with the teams responsible for operating facilities and site requirements.

  3. 03

    Connect the specialist work

    Coordinate the agreed environmental, decontamination and removal scope, including material handling and disposal needs.

  4. 04

    Document the handover

    Provide the agreed closeout information for owner review and the next stage of recovery, transition or redevelopment.

The people behind the decisions.

Site leadership and operations

Identify retained operations, access restrictions, work windows and the authority for site decisions.

Environmental and EHS teams

Coordinate known hazards, material information and applicable handling, transport and disposal requirements.

Engineering and asset owners

Define the intended end condition, operating interfaces and any required change-management or startup reviews.

  • Emergency recovery

  • Hazardous-material abatement
  • Decontamination
  • Selective demolition
  • Facility decommissioning
