Qualifications / Relevant capability

Follow the conditions through the work plan.

The site investigation, work sequence and intended finish should inform one another. Use four connected questions to organize the early discussion.

What is documented?

Review available investigations, sampling data and material records. Identify information gaps and the professionals responsible for interpreting conditions and defining technical requirements.

Where will materials go?

Discuss segregation, stockpiling, reuse and off-site pathways against the site's requirements. Coordinate handling areas and access with other workstreams.

How does water interact?

Bring drainage, water-management and dewatering requirements into the work discussion where relevant. Connect environmental responsibilities with the proposed civil activities.

What defines completion?

Establish restoration and acceptance requirements with the owner and relevant specialists. Define required records and verification responsibilities before the work reaches its final stage.

Owner conversation / Define the work

Turn the first meeting into a useful scope review.

Bring the environmental, operational and property perspectives together. The following inputs help the delivery discussion stay tied to the site's actual requirements.

  • Known conditions

    Provide investigations, test results, prior site uses and known affected areas. Distinguish confirmed findings from conditions still under evaluation.

  • Technical responsibilities

    Identify environmental consultants, engineers and approval authorities involved. Clarify who establishes requirements, verifies results and accepts the completed work.

  • Material and access routes

    Map available handling areas, retained operations and access limits. Discuss potential reuse or recovery only within the documented technical and project requirements.

  • Restoration and next use

    Describe the intended property condition, retained features and next phase. Agree on the records and site criteria needed for turnover.

Bring NorthStar the available site evidence and the outcome you need. A coordinated review can then connect remediation, materials and restoration to a defined scope.
