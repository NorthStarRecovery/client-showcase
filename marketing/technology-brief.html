Pentagon: adjacent experience in a complex facility

Phasedwork around active building services
Pentagon Renovations | 2001–2011

Pentagon renovations during 2001 to 2011 demonstrate adjacent government-facility experience in phased abatement and demolition around active services. This example does not establish direct data-center project experience for NorthStar.

01

The challenge

The Pentagon's 2001 to 2011 renovation program required abatement and interior demolition while military operations and essential building services continued. Security procedures and active systems passing through work areas shaped the project. This is adjacent government-facility experience, not a data-center engagement.

02

The work

NorthStar Group Services performed abatement and demolition across more than four million square feet. Crews separated occupied areas with hard walls and dust barriers, arranged tenant movements and worked through controlled access procedures. Phased scheduling preserved active mechanical, electrical and plumbing services that crossed work zones, with major system work assigned to off-peak periods.

03

The milestone

The project reported no mechanical, electrical or plumbing interruptions from NorthStar's work. That result belongs to this government-facility project; it is not a technology-site uptime commitment or evidence of direct data-center work.
