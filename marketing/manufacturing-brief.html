Manufacturing / From scope to handoff

A coordinated path through the work.

Define the physical recovery scope, connect the responsible teams and agree on the decisions needed at each stage.

  1. 01

    Agree the priorities

    Identify affected spaces, critical adjacencies, site access and the plant's immediate recovery objectives.

  2. 02

    Define the physical scope

    Assess building damage and known material concerns, then establish the remediation, removal and rebuilding work.

  3. 03

    Coordinate the work

    Sequence recovery activities with plant leadership and designated specialists, keeping scope decisions and progress documented.

  4. 04

    Support the handover

    Complete the agreed facility scope and coordinate the handoff for owner, OEM and quality signoffs on production restart.

The people behind the decisions.

Plant leadership

Sets production priorities, available work windows and the contacts authorized to make site decisions.

Engineering, OEMs and quality

Define equipment checks, product requirements and the acceptance decisions needed before production resumes.

EHS and risk management

Coordinate site hazards, work boundaries and the documentation needed by the wider recovery team.

Services to shape the scope

  • Pre-loss planning
  • Fire and smoke restoration
  • Water-damage recovery
  • Environmental remediation
  • Selective demolition
  • Reconstruction
