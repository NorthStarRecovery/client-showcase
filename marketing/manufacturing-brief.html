Firestone: restoring a path to production

23days to production restart
Firestone Polymers Plant | 2020

Following Hurricane Laura in August 2020, production began at Firestone's Lake Charles polymers plant after a 23-day recovery effort. That milestone describes restart; final project completion is unspecified.

01

The challenge

Hurricane Laura damaged more than 35 structures and critical support systems at Firestone's Lake Charles polymers plant in August 2020. With power lost across the site, the recovery team faced a 28-day target for the return of production.

02

The work

NorthStar's operations team arrived within six hours of landfall. Temporary generation supported continuous shifts involving more than 100 personnel. The work combined structural repairs, demolition of compromised structures and environmental remediation. A mobile office complex gave Firestone staff working space while repairs and recovery progressed across the plant.

03

The milestone

Production began after a 23-day recovery effort, ahead of the stated 28-day target. This was a production restart milestone; the project's final completion date is not specified.
