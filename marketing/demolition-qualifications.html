Qualifications / Relevant capability

Four capabilities to bring into the review.

Use the actual facility conditions to evaluate the work package. NorthStar's published service range supports a connected discussion across removal, recovery and restoration.

Plan the removal

Connect demolition planning, permitting interfaces and the proposed removal sequence with available structural information, site access and the owner's operating constraints.

Coordinate retained conditions

Identify utilities, neighboring operations and assets outside the removal scope. Establish the information and authorizations needed before each affected work area is released.

Integrate material management

Sequence hazardous-material abatement, salvage, recycling and disposal with demolition. The material condition and destination should inform handling decisions.

Deliver a defined finish

Align site restoration with the completion criteria. NorthStar's Air Products 2TX profile documents combined demolition, abatement and restoration during 2023-2024.

Qualifications / 03
Owner conversation / Define the work

Build a qualification package that supports a decision.

Procurement and project teams need evidence they can compare. Request information that shows how the proposed team will address this facility and this scope.

  • Relevant project examples

    Select experience by structure type, materials, operating interfaces and finish condition. A similar constraint can matter more than a similar contract value.

  • Proposed delivery team

    Identify leadership, supervision, specialist support and responsibility for each workstream. Confirm the contracting entity and the interfaces with owner-retained consultants.

  • Safety and controls

    Review the current safety program, proposed task controls and requested performance records with their reporting periods. Discuss utility isolation and other site-specific requirements.

  • Scope and closeout

    Document inclusions, exclusions, owner decisions, material destinations and acceptance criteria. Establish how changes in field conditions will enter the decision process.

Bring NorthStar your removal limits, retained conditions and intended site outcome. The qualification conversation can then focus on the work that will make the difference.
