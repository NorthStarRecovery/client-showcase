Relevant experience / The work behind the result

JLL / HSBC: recovery across a portfolio

Nationalportfolio program · 2009 to 2015
Jones Lang LaSalle / HSBC | 2009–2015

NorthStar's historical master service agreement with Jones Lang LaSalle and HSBC supported fire, water and disaster recovery across a national portfolio during 2009 to 2015. It is not a current-contract claim.

01

The challenge

Jones Lang LaSalle and HSBC needed recovery support across a national property portfolio. Regional storms and severe freezing weather could affect several locations at once, adding simultaneous property needs to local fire and water losses throughout the country.

02

The work

Under a master service agreement from 2009 through 2015, NorthStar supported fire, water and disaster recovery across the portfolio. Before Hurricane Sandy, the team worked with JLL and provided twice-daily weather updates. NorthStar then recovered multiple HSBC properties and later responded to pipe breaks during severe Northeast cold.

03

The milestone

The historical program supported recovery across multiple properties after regional events and local losses. The 2009 to 2015 period describes documented experience, rather than a current customer agreement or service guarantee.

Before the next interruption

Start with the property you know best.

Choose one property for a readiness discussion with its manager and building engineer. Use the building's actual access conditions, tenant responsibilities and approval process to make the conversation specific.

  1. Authority and approvals

    Who can authorize emergency work, approve changes and brief ownership on recovery decisions?

  2. Access and occupancy

    Which entrances, loading routes, occupied areas and working hours will shape the work?

  3. Tenant responsibilities

    Who owns each affected space, and who will keep tenants informed as recovery progresses?
