Commercial real estate / From scope to handoff

A coordinated path through the work.

Define the physical recovery scope, connect the responsible teams and agree on the decisions needed at each stage.

  1. 01

    Review the property

    Identify key contacts, access conditions and recovery priorities. Review available building information with the manager and engineer.

  2. 02

    Agree on the work

    Define the affected scope and work boundaries with the property team. Confirm how approvals and tenant-facing updates will be handled.

  3. 03

    Coordinate recovery

    Sequence restoration and necessary material removal around site conditions, access and the property team's operating priorities.

  4. 04

    Plan the reconstruction

    Bring repair needs and reconstruction options to ownership. Agree on the scope and handoff requirements for the affected spaces.

The people behind the decisions.

Owners and asset managers

Set recovery priorities, approve scope and distinguish immediate repairs from elective capital improvements.

Property teams and engineers

Coordinate access, building information, tenant updates and the operating conditions that shape the work.

NorthStar recovery team

Assess physical recovery needs and coordinate agreed restoration, remediation and reconstruction work.
