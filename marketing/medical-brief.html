Medical / From scope to handoff

A coordinated path through the work.

Define the physical recovery scope, connect the responsible teams and agree on the decisions needed at each stage.

  1. 01

    Review the facility

    Bring available building information and designated facility contacts together. Identify affected spaces and the priorities guiding recovery.

  2. 02

    Coordinate the scope

    Review proposed work with engineering and infection prevention. Align boundaries, access and sequencing with facility-directed requirements.

  3. 03

    Carry out agreed recovery

    Perform the agreed physical restoration and remediation work, coordinating with facility teams and relevant specialist contractors.

  4. 04

    Support the facility handoff

    Complete agreed repairs and provide work-status information. The facility's authorized teams determine the next steps for returning spaces to use.

The people behind the decisions.

Facilities and engineering

Guide building access, utility coordination, site information and the physical requirements for recovery.

Facility care and safety teams

Set infection-control requirements and care priorities, review proposed work and direct decisions about returning spaces to use.

NorthStar recovery team

Coordinate agreed restoration, remediation and repairs with the facility's designated teams and recovery process.
