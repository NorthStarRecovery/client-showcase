Relevant experience / The work behind the result

North Central Bronx: space for the next phase

100,000+square feet of preparatory work
North Central Bronx Hospital | 2020

At North Central Bronx Hospital in 2020, preparation across eight floors supported the wider ICU reconstruction program. The four-week intensive effort describes preparatory work, rather than completion of the clinical expansion.

01

The challenge

New York City's early 2020 COVID-19 surge created an urgent need for additional intensive-care space. At North Central Bronx Hospital, existing floors needed substantial preparation before reconstruction could move forward as part of the hospital's wider expansion program.

02

The work

NorthStar mobilized multiple shifts across more than 100,000 square feet and eight floors. Crews removed aged systems, interior finishes and unused spaces while addressing hazardous materials within the demolition work. An intensive four-week preparation effort supported the reconstruction sequence and required close work with the active healthcare facility.

03

The milestone

The four-week preparation effort supported the next phase of ICU reconstruction. The full clinical expansion formed part of the hospital's wider program, with its own clinical and operational objectives.

Before the next interruption

Bring facility requirements into the first conversation.

Invite facilities, infection prevention and emergency management to review the setting together. Begin with occupied areas, access needs and the work information each team needs to support facility-led decisions.

  1. Affected and adjacent

    Which departments and occupied spaces sit beside the proposed work, and what access restrictions apply?

  2. Utility and water contacts

    Who reviews building utilities and water-system concerns alongside physical restoration work?

  3. Facility handoff

    What records and completion reviews do the authorized facility teams need before deciding how space returns to use?
