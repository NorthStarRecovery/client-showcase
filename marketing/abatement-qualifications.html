Qualifications / Relevant capability

Four dimensions of an abatement qualification review.

Ask how the proposed scope responds to the materials and how the team will coordinate with the people responsible for the building and its construction program.

Documented material conditions

Identify the surveys, sampling information, locations and quantities available for pricing and planning. Establish how additional findings will be assessed and communicated.

Work-area requirements

Review proposed boundaries, access and containment needs for the specific material and setting. Coordinate building interfaces with the owner and relevant environmental professionals.

Sequence and supervision

Discuss how abatement will connect with selective demolition and adjacent work. Review proposed supervision and task planning against the site's safety requirements.

Testing and release

Define sampling, monitoring, clearance and documentation responsibilities where applicable. Agree on the evidence required before affected areas move into the next phase.

Owner conversation / Define the work

Make the qualification request match the setting.

A focused request helps owners, EHS teams and construction managers compare relevant evidence. Keep technical responsibilities visible throughout the review.

  • Material and project fit

    Request comparable experience for the identified materials and building conditions, including occupied or otherwise constrained settings when relevant.

  • Current scope credentials

    Request licenses, training records and other credentials required for the proposed location and work. Confirm the responsible delivery entity and personnel.

  • Facility coordination

    Identify facility contacts, environmental consultants and adjacent trades. Define responsibilities for access, work-area changes and communications affecting occupied areas.

  • Completion documentation

    Specify the required records and acceptance process, including applicable waste documentation, monitoring or clearance results and any owner-retained verification.

NorthStar's abatement qualifications should answer your material, building and handoff questions together. Begin with the available surveys and the conditions the work must respect.
