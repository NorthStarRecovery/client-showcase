Capabilities / Scope planning

Qualify the team for the work.

Connect relevant experience with the responsibilities your project requires. Review these four evidence areas with the proposed team.

Comparable work

Review projects with relevant materials, retained operations and end conditions. Air Products’ Texas City 2TX project combines demolition, abatement and restoration.

Delivery responsibilities

Identify the contracting entity, leadership and specialist roles. Confirm included services, separate engagements and how the interfaces will be managed.

Safety in execution

Review job safety analysis, supervision, training and task controls. Match requested performance records to the reporting period and delivery entity.

Technical breadth

Evaluate emergency response separately from planned work. Nuclear engagements require specialist scope, including the project’s waste and license-termination responsibilities.
